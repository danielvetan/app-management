Features

Everything NAVFIT98A checks. None of the hassle.

No installs, no Access database, no hunting for the right block. Just the questions that matter, on the device you already carry.

One question at a time

Plain steps walk you through every block of the EVAL, CHIEFEVAL and FITREP, with the right format shown as you type.

Every rule, checked

Built on BUPERSINST 1610.10H CH-2 and the NAVFIT98A v33 checks. Problems are flagged with a “Fix it” link before you print.

PSG RSCA, worked out

On E-5 and E-6 EVALs the last line of block 43 is kept for the RSCA. Enter your BOL numbers once and myEval does the math.

Summary groups

Group Sailors who are ranked together. Type the reporting senior and dates once, and every eval in the group stays in sync.

Hand off to NAVFIT98A

Export a file NAVFIT98A can bring in with its own Import Data, for commands that still route evals through NAVFIT.

Encrypt & send

Pass a report or a whole group to your rater or senior rater as a password-protected file, sealed with AES-256.

Print-ready forms

Prints the official layout, with comments counted the way they’ll print — not the way they look on your screen.

The instruction, built in

The full BUPERSINST 1610.10H text, searchable and offline. Tap any block to read its guidance.

App Lock with Face ID

Optional lock with Face ID or your passcode. SSNs are only kept when App Lock is on, and stay masked until you confirm it’s you.

How it works

From blank form to ready-to-sign.

Answer the questions

Start an EVAL, CHIEFEVAL or FITREP and fill it in step by step — on the ship, on the road, or between meetings.

Let myEval check it

Formats, limits, grade rules and the PSG RSCA are checked as you go. Anything that would stop the report is called out.

Print, send or export

Print the form, send an encrypted copy up the chain, or export for NAVFIT98A.

Privacy

Your evals stay on your device.

myEval has no servers, no sign-in and no analytics. We never see your reports — they’re stored encrypted on your iPhone or iPad and leave it only when you choose to share them.

Read the privacy policy →

  • Encrypted drafts. Reports are saved with AES-256 encryption, with the key kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • SSNs protected. Optional, never required, and only stored when App Lock is on.
  • No tracking. No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs that phone home.
